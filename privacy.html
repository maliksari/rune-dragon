Rune Dragon – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-13

Rune Dragon does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data. The game works fully offline: it has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDKs.

Your progress (levels, stars, settings, language) is stored only in the app's local storage on your device and is removed when you delete the app.
